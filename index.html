The Ako Movement

Isang kilusan ng mga batang mamamahayag — from campus pubs to press cons nationwide.

PANEL 1 — ORIGIN

Campus Journalist Ako started as a rallying cry for student writers, photographers, and layout artists who wanted more than a school paper deadline — they wanted a real newsroom culture, built for and by campus press.

Today it's a nationwide network of desks: News, Features, Sports, Sci-Tech, and Opinion, mentoring the next wave of Filipino journalists from Luzon to Mindanao.
